The Visionary

My name is Amy. I am a web developer and numerologist from Springfield, Missouri. My journey started in 1990, traveling 1,100 miles with a newborn, a baby, a toddler, and a six-year-old. I learned then that life isn't about what happens to you—it's about the frequency you choose to embody during the move.

I created Sunny Daze to bridge the gap between technical logic and spiritual energy. Using JavaScript, React, and Node.js to build tools that decipher the codes of life. Whether I am troubleshooting a 2048 zero-turn mower or a complex line of code, the principle is the same: find the break in the circuit and restore the flow.
