Frequency Mapping: It’s combining your intentions with the conscious mind to lock in the new reality. You use intentions to trigger the leap.

Phase One

Phase one is all about building up your vibration by using frequencies 432, 528, 963 and others. Listen to these every night (head phones for best results), while sleeping. For as long as you feel the need to before progressing onto phase two. I recommend 21 to 30 days. This will help raise your conscious vibration. Phase one is a stand alone procedure, in that it helps you physically and mentally feel better. Raising your vibration makes a person happy, calm and able to handle life’s circumstances in a positive way. This can be done by shifters and non-shifters.

432hz

This is an alternative music note for A. Also known as Nature’s frequency. A 2020 study found that it improves mood and reduces symptoms of anxiety. It is believed to help with mental focus and clarity.

528hz

Known as the love frequency. It is believed that the tone promotes relaxation, reduces stress, and helps in DNA cell repair. Research shows that the frequency lowers cortisol levels and boosts oxytocin.

963hz

Also known as the frequency of the Gods. This tone is used to activate the pineal gland, open the crown chakra. It enhances mental clarity and calms the mind. Promotes a feeling of returning to your original state.

Phase Two

Now that your vibration is high enough to leap it is time for the reprogramming. You will need a good set of cushioned head phones and a place to lie down where you will not be disturbed. On a daily or nightly basis listen to a deep sleep hypnosis audio. Specifically tailored towards reprogramming the subconscious mind. It doesn’t matter which ones you choose and their are plenty of free ones available on youtube.

Once you get comfortable and before you start the audio set your intentions. What is it you are needing? What would you like to be doing in your new reality? How would you like to be living? Really narrow it down. Pick one main difference and focus all your intentions on that change. Either say it out loud or in your head. This sets it up for your subconscious to learn. Now turn on the audio and follow the guided hypnosis. It is recommended to do this process every night until you start to notice changes.

Phase two is a major process. Don’t be discouraged if you don’t experience any changes right away. The conscious mind can be stubborn. It is having to relearn a whole new way of doing things. Since we were born we have been conditioned on how to look, act, think and even dream by our parents, elders, peers, and society. If you have been told all your life this is the way it is and nothing can be done about it. Your subconscious will push that narrative and your waking conscious mind will act upon it. That is why phase two of my method is a very important step.

Once you start to feel a difference in the way you carry yourself. You should start to see that others are noticing something different in you as well. They may even go as far as to say so. Its time to advance to phase three.

Phase Three: The Harmonic Integration

Now it is time to start using your subconscious to your advantage. Phase three was designed just for that reason. Now that you have reprogrammed your subconscious to start making your conscious mind behave in the way you are training it. You are ready for the “Mention Technique.” This technique will guide in manifesting the shift.

What you will do on a weekly basis is write down everything that you want this reality to be. But you have to write it like it already happened. You will be journaling things that you want to happen in a way that reads as if they already did. Really feel like it has happened. Carry yourself like you are living the life you always dreamed. And most importantly be very grateful.

1. Find time throughout your day to smile and say hi to people whether you know them or not.

2. Help someone in need if you are able.

3. Hold the door open or tell someone they look nice. Warm gestures are a key way of keeping that vibration high.

The happier you are the higher you will vibrate. The higher you vibrate the more you will manifest into your reality.

Soon you will start noticing things that you ask for out loud will seemingly appear for you. Your dream job gets offered to you. A promotion comes through out of the blue.
